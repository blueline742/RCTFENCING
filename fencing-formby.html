What Matters for Fencing in Formby

Local knowledge that makes a fence last

Working around mature trees

Many Formby gardens back onto pines and mature trees. We plan post positions to avoid cutting through major roots, and we adjust the spacing where we need to rather than damaging a tree.

Privacy for bigger gardens

For larger plots we often recommend 6ft feather edge on concrete posts. It is solid, private and long-lasting, and it does not need replacing every few years like budget panels.

Wildlife-friendly options

If you would like to keep a route open for hedgehogs, we can leave a small gap at the base of your fence or fit a gravel board with a hedgehog hole. Just ask when we quote.

Fencing in Formby: FAQ

Common questions from Formby customers

Can you put a fence up near trees without harming them?

+

Yes. We position posts to avoid large roots and hand-dig where needed. If a tree is protected, or you are in a conservation area, check with Sefton Council before any work near it.

What is the most private fence for a Formby garden?

+

A 6ft (1.8m) feather edge fence on concrete posts gives full privacy and lasts well. Panel fencing is a cheaper alternative. We will show you both when we quote.

Can you leave a gap for hedgehogs?

+

Yes. A 13cm gap at the base, or a gravel board with a purpose-made hedgehog hole, lets them move between gardens without making your fence any less secure.
