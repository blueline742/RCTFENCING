What Matters for Fencing in Maghull

Local knowledge that makes a fence last

Upgrading new-build fences

Builders often fit thin panels on timber posts that start to fail within a few years. We replace them with concrete posts, gravel boards and stronger panels or feather edge that will last.

Estate rules and front gardens

Many newer estates have covenants about front boundaries and open-plan front gardens. It is worth checking your deeds before changing a front fence, and we can work to whatever height or style is allowed.

Canal and field-edge gardens

Gardens backing onto the Leeds and Liverpool Canal or open fields often need taller, more secure boundaries. We can fit solid fencing or post-and-rail and stock fencing, depending on what you need.

Fencing in Maghull: FAQ

Common questions from Maghull customers

Can you replace the fence the builder put in on my new-build?

+

Yes, this is one of our most common Maghull jobs. We take out the old panels and posts and fit concrete posts, gravel boards and stronger fencing. The old materials are removed and disposed of.

Am I allowed to put a fence on my open-plan front garden?

+

That depends on your estate. Many new-build developments have covenants that limit front boundaries, so check your deeds or ask the developer first. Rear and side fences up to 2m normally do not need planning permission.

How long does a typical garden fence take to install?

+

Most residential fences are finished in one to three days, depending on length and access. We will give you a timeline when we quote.
