What Matters for Fencing in Crosby

Local knowledge that makes a fence last

Built for the wind off the coast

Crosby takes the full force of westerly winds coming in off the Irish Sea. Cheap panels on timber posts are the first thing to go in a winter storm, so for exposed gardens we recommend concrete posts and gravel boards, or a slatted design that lets the wind through.

Sandy ground near the beach

Closer to the shore, the ground is soft and sandy. We dig deeper post holes and use more concrete so the fence stays straight, rather than leaning after its first winter.

Older houses, tight access

Plenty of Crosby homes only have access through the house or down a narrow side passage. We are used to carrying materials through carefully, and we take every bit of the old fence away with us.

Fencing in Crosby: FAQ

Common questions from Crosby customers

How quickly can you come out to Crosby for a quote?

+

We are based in Waterloo, a few minutes from Crosby, so we can usually visit within the same week. Quotes and site visits are always free.

Which fence stands up best to the wind near Crosby beach?

+

For exposed gardens we recommend concrete posts with gravel boards, plus either heavy-duty feather edge or a slatted fence that lets some of the wind pass through. We will look at how exposed your garden is and advise honestly.

Do I need permission for a front fence in Crosby?

+

Fences up to 1 metre at the front and 2 metres at the back and sides normally do not need planning permission. Some streets may be in a conservation area, so if you are unsure, check with Sefton Council. We are happy to help you work out what applies.
