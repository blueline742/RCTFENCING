Privacy Policy

How RCT Fencing LTD handles your personal information

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Who we are

This website is run by RCT Fencing LTD (company number 15471433), 122 South Rd, Waterloo, Liverpool L22 0ND. We are the data controller for personal information collected through this website. Contact us about anything in this policy at info@rctfencing.co.uk or on 07545 342411.

What we collect

  • Enquiry forms: your name, email address, phone number (optional) and the details of your project that you type into the message box.
  • Phone, email and WhatsApp: whatever you choose to tell us when you contact us directly, including your address if you want a site visit.
  • Customers: your name, address, contact details and job and payment records once we carry out work for you.

We do not use analytics or advertising tracking on this website.

How we use it and why

  • To reply to your enquiry, arrange a site visit and give you a quote. This is a step you ask us to take before entering into a contract.
  • To carry out and invoice the work you book. This is needed to perform our contract with you.
  • To keep business and tax records. This is a legal obligation.

We never sell your information or use it for marketing lists.

Who we share it with

We only share your details with services that help us run the business:

  • Web3Forms, which delivers website form submissions to our inbox.
  • Our email provider (Namecheap Private Email), which stores our emails.
  • WhatsApp (Meta), if you choose to message us there.
  • Netlify, which hosts this website and may log technical information such as IP addresses for security.
  • Our accountant, and HMRC where the law requires it.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they use legally recognised safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

Third-party content on this site

Our pages load fonts and icons from Google Fonts and cdnjs/unpkg, and our home and contact pages show an embedded Google Map. These services receive your IP address when the page loads, and Google may set its own cookies through the map. See Google's privacy policy for details.

Cookies and local storage

This website does not set any cookies of its own. If you use the light/dark mode switch, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it. That information never leaves your device.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry. For customers, we keep job and payment records for six years, as UK tax rules require.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you can ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, object to how we use it, or ask us to restrict its use. To do any of this, email info@rctfencing.co.uk. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
